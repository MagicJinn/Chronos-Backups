What it does

Chronos is a serverside Minecraft backup utility. It prunes aged chunks so backups stay small while preserving the parts of the world that matter.

  • Runs on Fabric, NeoForge, Forge, and Bukkit/Spigot/Paper/Purpur/Folia across a wide version range.
  • Scheduled or manual backups via /chronos, with configurable retention and compression.
  • Optional cloud sync to Google Drive, including remote retention and dropping local copies after upload.
  • Rust-native chunk pruning for speed, with Docker and CI coverage across many loader/version builds.
